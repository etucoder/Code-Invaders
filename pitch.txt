Name : Lego Duplicator, Assembler, and Sorter (Factory)

I’m Building a 3D-Assembler / CNC Machine,using the Raspberry Pi 5. The machine will have a special function:
take any LEGO, and put it on the duplicator plate, and the machine will duplicate it to the best of 
its ability (Re-print it, colors may not be same) . The hardware will be built myself, and there is no premade tutorial. The machine will scan the brick,
you will enter the amount onto the Raspberry Pi , it will show you the time taken, a filament and cost estimate, and ask you which
filament to use. It will print the bricks, then they will be transferred to a assembly line , which will automatically sort them using a custom one-shot YOLO
model. If you chose to do so, the automatic, custom-build 3d-ASSEMBLER will assemble the legos into a build for you.

Inspo : I'm always 1-2 pieces short of a specific piece I want. Wouldn't it be great to be able to duplicate legos easily? (Plus its just so cool)

Past Projects : I've worked on a Raspberry Pi Robot and a 70-hour game called Code-Invaders, and also 3+ years 3d modeling before I knew about hackclub...

Why this is worth $200+ : This is a custom, innovative project that will help me master my hardware skills.
This project will also be incredibly useful for anyone who wants to duplicate LEGOs or just build something cool...
Its a mimic of a perfessional build for a (relativly ) small price that is incredibly unique.
